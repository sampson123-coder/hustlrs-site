How it works

Pick a mate. Set a target. Walk.

Every challenge runs Monday to Sunday. Your steps count themselves, so all you have to do is move.

1

Challenge a friend

Pick a mate, set a weekly steps target like 70,000, and choose a forfeit for whoever loses.

2

Walk all week

Your iPhone or Apple Watch counts your steps automatically through Apple Health. No logging, no check-ins.

3

Loser pays up

Most steps on Sunday night wins. If you both hit the target it's a draw and nobody owes. The loser proves the forfeit with a photo taken in the app.

Why steps

The simplest habit that works.

Walking more is one of the best things you can do for your body and your head. The average person manages about 5,000 steps a day.3

The honest FAQ

Questions you're thinking.

Is it free?

Yes. Hustlrs is free on iPhone, and no money changes hands in the app. The forfeit is between you and your mate.

How are my steps counted?

Your iPhone or Apple Watch counts them automatically through Apple Health. Hustlrs only reads your steps, it never writes to Apple Health.

What if we both hit the target?

Then it's a draw and nobody owes anything. If either of you misses, the most steps wins.

How do I know they paid up?

The loser proves the forfeit with a photo taken in the app, so there's no wriggling out of it.
