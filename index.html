The honest FAQ

Questions you're thinking.

Is this gambling?

No. You deposit money on your own behaviour, which is entirely in your control: complete your activities and your deposit is safe regardless of what anyone else does. No chance, no odds, no house against you. It's a commitment device, and it's 18+ only.

Where does my forfeit go in solo?

70% of whatever you forfeit is donated to support children's education through the Seeds of Africa Fund. The remaining 30% keeps Hustlrs running. So a missed day still does some good.

Friends or solo, which do I pick?

Friends if you want rivalry and a pot to win off your mates. Solo if it's just you, no group needed, you check in by location and anything you forfeit is donated. You can run both at once.

What if everyone hits their target?

Everyone gets every penny back and you all win. That's the point!

Can't people fake it?

In friends mode, photos are taken live in-app and the whole group sees them, so anyone can flag a fake and a majority flag means it doesn't count. In solo mode there's nothing to fake: the check-in is GPS-verified, so you have to actually be at the place.

How does my money move?

Charged to your card by Stripe when you join. When it ends, your share plus any winnings land in your in-app wallet, and you can withdraw to your bank anytime. Hustlrs never sees your card or bank details.

What if I only complete half my activities?

You get half your deposit back. The maths is always proportional: complete 75%, keep 75%. Your forfeit goes to the winner, or supports children's education in solo.
