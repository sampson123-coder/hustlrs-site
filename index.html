The honest FAQ

Questions you're thinking.

Is this gambling?

No. You stake money on your own behaviour, which is entirely in your control: complete your sessions and your deposit is safe regardless of what anyone else does. No chance, no odds, no house against you. It's a commitment device, and it's 18+ only.

What if everyone hits their target?

Everyone gets every penny back and you all win. That's the point.

How does my money move?

Charged to your card by Stripe when you join. When it ends, your share plus any winnings land in your in-app wallet, and you can withdraw to your bank anytime. Hustlrs never sees your card or bank details.

Can't people fake their photos?

Photos are taken live in-app, with a cooldown so you can't post a week's worth at once. And the group sees every photo, so anyone can flag one that isn't genuine, and a majority means it doesn't count.
