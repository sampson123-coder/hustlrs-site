How it works

Four steps. No excuses.

1

Everyone in a hustle stakes the same deposit and declares their weekly sessions. £5 minimum, your crew, your rules.

2

Prove it with a photo

Every session needs a photo, taken live from the camera. Cooldowns stop anyone faking a week in one night.

3

Your crew keeps you honest

Photos that break the rules get flagged. If the majority agrees by week's end, it doesn't count.

4

Winner takes the pot

Earn back what you complete. The best finisher takes the forfeits, paid straight to their wallet.

What's a "hustle"? A one-week money pool between friends. The host sets the deposit, start date and photo rules, then shares a 6-digit PIN. Everyone who joins pays the same deposit and declares their own weekly session target. When the week ends, the app settles the money automatically.

Questions you're already thinking

The honest FAQ.

Is this gambling?

No. You're staking money on your own behaviour, which is entirely in your control: complete your sessions and your deposit is safe regardless of what anyone else does. There's no chance, no odds, no house edge against you. It's a commitment device, like apps such as StepBet, and it's 18+ only.

What happens if everyone hits their target?

Everyone gets every penny back and Hustlrs takes nothing. And you all hit your goals! That's the point.

How does my money move?

Your deposit is charged to your card by Stripe when you join. When the week ends, the app settles automatically: the share you earned plus any winnings land in your in-app wallet, and you can withdraw to your bank whenever you like. Hustlrs never sees your card or bank details.

Can't people just fake their photos?

Photos are taken live from the in-app camera, with a cooldown so you can't post a week's worth in one evening. And your group sees every photo: if it breaks the host's rules, anyone can flag it, and a majority vote means it doesn't count.

Who decides the rules?

The host. They set the deposit, the start date, whether activities are fixed for everyone or flexible per person, and the photo rules, like "mirror selfie at the gym, face visible". Everything is locked once the hustle starts, and you see all of it before you put money in.

What if I only manage half my sessions?

You get half your deposit back. The maths is always proportional: complete 75%, keep 75%. Your forfeit goes to the winner's pot, or to the chosen charity in charity mode.
