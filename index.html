The honest FAQ

Questions you're thinking.

Is this gambling?

No. You stake money on your own behaviour, which is entirely in your control: complete your activities and your deposit is safe regardless of what anyone else does. No chance, no odds, no house against you. It's a commitment device, and it's 18+ only.

What if everyone hits their target?

Everyone gets every penny back and you all win. That's the point!

How does my money move?

Charged to your card by Stripe when you join. When it ends, your share plus any winnings land in your in-app wallet, and you can withdraw to your bank anytime. Hustlrs never sees your card or bank details.

Can't people fake their photos?

Photos are taken live in-app, with a cooldown so you can't post them all at once. And the group sees every photo, so anyone can flag one that isn't genuine, and a majority means it doesn't count.

What if someone flags my photo but it's real?

One flag does nothing on its own. Your photo stays live and keeps counting unless more than half the group has flagged it by the time the hustle ends. You're notified the moment a flag lands, the photo appears in the group chat where you can defend it, and flaggers can take their flag back once you've cleared it up. Worst case, delete and retake. One salty mate can't cost you money.

Who decides the setup?

The host. They set the deposit, the start and end dates, and the activities everyone follows. Everything is locked once the hustle starts, and you see all of it before you put money in.

What if I only complete half my activities?

You get half your deposit back. The maths is always proportional: complete 75%, keep 75%. Your forfeit goes into the pot for the winner.
