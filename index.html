How it works

Four steps. No excuses.

1

Put money on it

Everyone in a hustle stakes the same deposit and declares their weekly sessions. £5 minimum, your crew, your rules.

2

Prove it with a photo

Every session needs a photo, taken live from the camera. Cooldowns stop anyone faking a week in one night.

3

Your crew keeps you honest

Photos that break the rules get flagged. If the majority agrees by week's end, it doesn't count.

4

Winner takes the pot

Earn back what you complete. The best finisher takes the forfeits, paid straight to their wallet.

The pot

Every missed session funds someone else's grind.

You get back exactly the share of your target you complete. Whatever you don't earn stays in the pot, and the member with the highest completion takes it. Everyone hits 100%? Everyone gets every penny back.

Deposits, wallets and payouts run on Stripe. We never see your card.

Keep each other honest

No refs. Just your mates and a flag button.

Hosts set the photo rules: mirror selfie at the gym, face visible, whatever keeps it real. A dodgy photo gets flagged anonymously, the group sees it in chat, and the poster can defend it or retake it.

If more than half the hustle flags it, it doesn't count. Democracy, but for burpees.
