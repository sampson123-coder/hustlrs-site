Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Hustlrs is a free app that helps you keep the workouts you plan. Each week you plan your sessions, then check in when you do them to earn points and titles, and your friends see how you are doing. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, who can see it, and what your rights are. Hustlrs is based in the UK and we handle your data in line with UK GDPR.

Who we are

Hustlrs is the data controller for your personal data. You can reach us at hustlrsapp@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and sign-in details. You can sign in with Apple, Google or email. If you use Apple or Google, we receive your email address from them (or Apple's private relay address if you choose to hide it).
  • Profile: your display name and your @username. If you added a profile photo in an earlier version of the app, it is still stored at a public web address until you remove it or delete your account.
  • Your Hustlr: the look you choose for your avatar (skin tone, hair style and hair colour) and your time zone, so your days and weeks start at the right time.
  • Your plan: for each session you plan, the day, the activity (for example Gym or Football) and, if you add one, the place: its name and its map coordinates. We also keep whether each session was kept or skipped, how you checked in, and when.
  • Location when you check in: if you check in at your place, the app gets your location once, only when you tap to check in and only while the app is open. It sends those coordinates to our servers to confirm you are at the place you planned. We do not store your check-in location and we do not track your location in the background.
  • Check-in photos: if you check in with a photo, the app checks the photo on your iPhone, then stores it in private storage that only you can open. Your friends cannot see your check-in photos.
  • Points and titles: the points you earn and lose for kept and skipped sessions, and the title they give you each year (Rookie, Regular, Committed, Machine or Hustlr).
  • Apple Health: with your permission, we read your workouts and your daily step count. See the Apple Health section below.
  • Motion & Fitness: while the app is open, we use your iPhone's motion data to show your live step count. This stays on your device and is not sent to us.
  • Friends: the people you send friend requests to, accept, remove or block.
  • Notifications: a device token from Apple so we can send you push notifications, such as friend requests. Your plan reminders are set on your iPhone from your plan and are not sent through our servers.
  • Hustlrs Pro: if you subscribe, whether your subscription is active and when it ends, so the app knows to unlock Pro. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card details.
  • App usage: events like finishing sign-up, sending a friend request or opening the Pro page, linked to your account ID. See PostHog below.
  • Reports and feedback: if you report someone or send us a bug report or feature suggestion, we receive your message along with your username.
  • Earlier versions: earlier versions of Hustlrs had step challenges with friends (Hustles). If you used them, the data from them (step targets, forfeits, results and forfeit photos) stays in your account until you delete it.

Apple Health data

We take your health data seriously. In short:

  • We read only your workouts and your daily step counts. Access is read-only and we never write anything to Apple Health.
  • Workouts: when a workout you recorded today can count for a planned session, the app sends only the workout's ID to our servers, so the same workout cannot be used twice. The workout's other details stay on your iPhone.
  • Steps: the app sends one number per day (your total steps for that day) to our servers.
  • We never use Apple Health data for advertising or marketing, and we never sell it.
  • We never share Apple Health data with third parties, other than storing it with our hosting provider, Supabase, so the app can work.
  • You can turn off Hustlrs' access at any time in the iPhone Settings app under Health, Data Access & Devices.

What we use your data for

  • Running your account and signing you in.
  • Running your plan: confirming check-ins, working out your points and titles, and reminding you about your sessions.
  • Showing your Hustlr, title, points and sessions kept this week to your friends.
  • Sending notifications about your plan and friend requests. You can turn these off in Settings.
  • Keeping Hustlrs safe: handling reports, blocks and misuse.
  • Fixing bugs and improving the app, using the feedback you send us and app usage events (see PostHog below).

We do not sell your data and we do not show third-party adverts.

Our legal bases under UK GDPR are: performing our contract with you (running the app you signed up for), your explicit consent (for reading your data from Apple Health and for using your location when you check in, which you can withdraw at any time), and our legitimate interests (keeping the app safe and improving it).

Who can see what

  • Anyone using Hustlrs can find you by your display name or @username.
  • Your friends can see your display name, @username, your Hustlr, your title, your points and how many sessions you kept this week. They never see the sessions you skipped, your plan, your places, your check-in photos or your location.
  • Only you can see your plan, your places and your check-in photos.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase, our hosting provider, stores our database, sign-in system and photos.
  • Apple delivers push notifications through the Apple Push Notification service, handles Sign in with Apple, and provides the maps and place search you use to add a place to your plan.
  • Google handles sign-in if you choose Sign in with Google.
  • PostHog (hosted in the EU) gives us product analytics: which screens and features get used, so we know what to improve. Events are tied to your account ID only, never your name or email. We do not record your screen, and we never use it for advertising or to track you across other apps.
  • Apple In-App Purchase: if you buy Hustlrs Pro, Apple handles the payment. We never see your card details.

We may also share data if the law requires it.

Waiting list

If you join the waiting list on hustlrs.co, we store your email address and the date you joined. We use it only to email you when Hustlrs is available on the App Store. We do not share it or use it for anything else. We delete the list after the launch email, and you can ask us to remove your email at any time.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from Settings in the app. When you do, we permanently delete your account, profile, Hustlr, plan, places, points, check-in photos, step data and any data from earlier versions, including forfeit photos you posted. We may keep a small amount of data where the law requires it.

Children

You must be 16 or over to use Hustlrs. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you think a child under 16 is using Hustlrs, please email us and we will delete their account.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, receive a copy of it, and object to or restrict how we use it. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. To use any of these rights, email us. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make important changes, we will let you know in the app.

Contact

For anything privacy related, email hustlrsapp@gmail.com.
