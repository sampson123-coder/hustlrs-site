What Hustlrs is

Hustlrs is a fitness commitment platform. You deposit money against your own declared workout target and earn it back by completing sessions, proven with photos. Hustlrs is a test of your own behaviour, not a game of chance: you alone control whether you complete your sessions.

Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old and a UK resident to use Hustlrs with real money.

Deposits, refunds and payouts

  • Your deposit is charged when you join or create a hustle. Once a hustle starts, deposits are committed and cannot be withdrawn early.
  • You earn back the share of your deposit matching the share of your weekly target you complete. The remainder is forfeited to the pot.
  • In stakes mode, the member with the highest completion rate (minimum 50% of their own target) wins 70% of the forfeited pot; Hustlrs retains 30% as its platform fee. If nobody reaches 50%, the 70% is donated to charity. If everyone completes their target, all deposits are returned in full and no fee is taken.
  • In charity mode, 90% of forfeits go to the hustle's chosen charity and 10% to Hustlrs.
  • Winnings and refunds are credited to your in-app wallet and can be withdrawn to your bank via Stripe after a one-time identity verification.

Fair play

  • Session photos must follow the rules set by the hustle's host. Members can flag photos that don't; a photo flagged by more than half the hustle does not count.
  • Attempting to cheat (fake photos, duplicate posts, manipulated submissions) may result in removal from hustles or account termination, with forfeiture of pending stakes.
