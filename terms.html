What Hustlrs is

Hustlrs is a free app that helps you keep the workouts you plan. Each week you plan your sessions, then check in when you do them. Done sessions earn points, and your points give you a title for the year. You never lose points. Your friends see your Hustlr, your title and your points.

Who can use Hustlrs

You must be at least 16 years old to use Hustlrs. You need an iPhone. Checking in with a workout needs Apple Health.

Your account

  • You can sign up with Apple, Google or email. Keep your login details safe. You are responsible for what happens on your account.
  • Your display name and @username must not be offensive or pretend to be someone else. You can change your @username once every 30 days.
  • You can delete your account at any time in Settings. This permanently deletes your account, profile, plan, points and check-in photos.

Your plan, check-ins and points

  • You plan your week in the app: an activity for each day and, if you like, the place you will do it.
  • You check in at your session's location, with a photo, or with a workout from Apple Health. A done session earns 1 point. A planned session you do not check in for is missed and earns no points. You never lose points.
  • Full-week bonus: if you plan 2 or more sessions in a week and complete all of them, you earn 2 extra points. Skip days do not stop the bonus.
  • Skip days: you can turn a planned session today or later this week into a skip day. A skip day earns no points and does not break your week. Free accounts get 2 skip days a month and Pro gets 6.
  • Session changes: free accounts can change 1 planned session a month and Pro can change 4.
  • Free accounts count 1 session a day and Pro counts up to 3, so Pro members can earn more points in a day. Sessions on the same day must be different activities. Free accounts get 2 photo check-ins a week and Pro gets unlimited photo check-ins.
  • Your points give you a title: Rookie (0), Regular (50), Committed (130), Machine (230) or Hustlr (350). Titles start again from Rookie on 1 January.
  • Check-ins depend on your phone's location, camera and Apple Health. These can sometimes be wrong or late, and we cannot guarantee that every check-in works.
  • Earlier versions of Hustlrs had step challenges with forfeits (Hustles). These are switched off. No money changes hands in Hustlrs.
