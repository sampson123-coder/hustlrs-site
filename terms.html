What Hustlrs is

Hustlrs is a free app for step challenges with your friends. You add friends, see each other's weekly steps, and challenge each other to weekly 1v1 Hustles. Your steps come from Apple Health.

You must be at least 18 years old to use Hustlrs. You need an iPhone with Apple Health to take part in step challenges.

  • You can delete your account at any time in Settings. This permanently deletes your account, profile, steps and Hustle data.

Hustles

  • A Hustle is a weekly 1v1 step challenge between you and a friend. It runs from Monday to Sunday and has a step target.
  • Each Hustle has a forfeit: a friendly dare or treat the loser does, like buying the winner a Nando's. Forfeits are up to 30 characters.
  • The loser posts a photo taken with the in-app camera as proof they did the forfeit. Their opponent can see it. The photo can be retaken for 15 minutes after it is posted, then it is locked.
  • Results are based on the step counts from Apple Health. Step counts can sometimes be delayed or inaccurate, and we cannot guarantee they are perfect.
