What Hustlrs is

Hustlrs is a commitment platform. You put a deposit on your own declared targets and keep the share you complete, proven by checking in at real locations. Hustlrs is a test of your own behaviour, not a game of chance: you alone control whether you complete your activities, and no member can ever win another member's money.

Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old and a UK resident to use Hustlrs with real money.

Deposits and charges

  • When you join or create a hustle, your card is saved with Stripe. Nothing is charged at that point, and nothing is charged when the hustle starts.
  • By joining, you authorise Hustlrs to charge your saved card after the hustle ends for the forfeited share of your deposit, without further action from you.
  • At the end of the hustle, you are charged only for the share of your target you did not complete. Complete 75% and you are charged 25% of your deposit. Complete everything and you are charged nothing.
  • Your outcome depends only on your own completion. Forfeited money is never paid to other members and there is no pot to win.
  • Of the money collected from forfeits, 70% is donated to the Seeds of Africa Fund, which supports children's education, and 30% is retained by Hustlrs as its platform fee. Donations are passed on to the fund on a monthly schedule.
  • If a charge fails, we may retry it via our payment processor.

Joining, leaving and cancellation

  • A hustle's terms (activities, targets, deposit, dates) are locked once it is created. Where you do each activity is yours: every member pins their own location for each activity, and those locations are visible to the other members of the hustle.
  • You can leave a hustle at any time before it starts. Nothing has been charged, so leaving costs nothing.
  • If a friends hustle does not have at least two members by its start time, it is cancelled automatically and no one is charged. The host can also cancel a hustle before it starts.
  • Once a hustle starts you are committed for the week and cannot leave.

Fair play

  • Each activity's proof is set by the host: a photo taken at the pinned location, a GPS check-in alone, or a live photo alone. Where an activity has a pinned location, you must actually be there for the check-in to count, and the camera only unlocks there. Photos must always be taken live in the app.
  • Attempting to cheat (spoofing your location, manipulated submissions, or misrepresenting an activity's location) may result in removal from hustles or account termination. You remain liable for forfeits already incurred.
