What Hustlrs is

Hustlrs is a commitment platform. You deposit money against your own declared targets and earn it back by completing your activities, proven with photos. Hustlrs is a test of your own behaviour, not a game of chance: you alone control whether you complete your activities.

Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old and a UK resident to use Hustlrs with real money.

Deposits, refunds and payouts

  • Your deposit is charged when you join or create a hustle. Once a hustle starts, deposits are committed and cannot be withdrawn early.
  • You earn back the share of your deposit matching the share of your target you complete. The remainder is forfeited to the pot.
  • The member with the highest completion rate wins 70% of the forfeited pot; Hustlrs retains 30% as its platform fee. If everyone completes their target, all deposits are returned in full and no fee is taken.
  • Winnings and refunds are credited to your in-app wallet and can be withdrawn to your bank via Stripe after a one-time identity verification.

Fair play

  • Activity photos must be genuine and taken live in the app. Members can flag photos that aren't; a photo flagged by more than half the hustle does not count.
  • Attempting to cheat (fake photos, duplicate posts, manipulated submissions) may result in removal from hustles or account termination, with forfeiture of pending deposits.
